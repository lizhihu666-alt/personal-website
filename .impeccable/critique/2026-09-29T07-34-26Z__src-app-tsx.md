## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Not a generic dev-portfolio skin. The two-door home entrance (single continuous AeroShards particle field that hard-cuts from work-green to life-blue at the midline), the pixel-dissolve scroll transitions, the three.js "四条能力路径汇成一条线" convergence, and the EXIF-styled pet-photography showcase are all built around this person's specific 职业/生活 duality, not a template with swapped text. Copy is concrete and first-person, not marketing-generic.

**Deterministic scan**: `impeccable detect --json src` exited 2 with 4 findings, all the same rule -- `overused-font` (Geist, flagged at `src/fonts.css:28/38` and `src/index.css:51/157`). Browser-side direct API scans corroborated this at 95% of text on Work and 62% on Life. This doesn't contradict the specificity verdict on layout/interaction, but it does mean the one place the site is currently not distinctive is typography.

Browser scans also surfaced real, unrelated legibility defects: on Work, `low-contrast` x4 (3.0:1 vs required 4.5:1, `#8A94A3` on `#FBFCFD`) and `dark-glow` x5 (zero-offset shadows on the green accent, likely intentional but worth a look); on Life, `tiny-text` x7 (down to 11px) and `undersized-ui-text` x4 (10px labels: FOCAL/APERTURE/ISO/LEO PET ARCHIVE), plus `cramped-padding` x1 and `nested-cards` x6 (mostly the travel-ticket cards).

**Visual overlays**: Script injection and the detector both ran successfully against the live page (confirmed via direct `impeccableScan()` calls, not just console scraping) across all three views. No visual overlay was left persisted in the browser -- the sub-agent's tab and supporting live-server were already closed out by the time this was reported.

## Overall Impression

The direction is genuinely bespoke and the ending (WorkContact) is well-executed. The single biggest problem is that the site's heaviest craft investment -- the pinned scrollytelling on the Work side -- works against the product's own success metric ("recruiter judges in minutes"), and on mobile it currently breaks outright on first load. The Work side needs a legibility and restraint pass; the Life side is in noticeably better shape.

## What's Working

1. **WorkContact section** -- exact match to PRODUCT.md's constrained contact surface (email/WeChat/LinkedIn/GitHub only, no phone), with copy-to-clipboard removing friction at the single highest-stakes moment on the site.
2. **Life photography section** (`LifeView.tsx` `#life-photography`) -- real photo with EXIF-styled metadata, keyboard-navigable tabs (arrow keys + roving tabindex), genuinely distinct tone from Work.
3. **Home entrance** (`HomeHero.tsx`) -- one shared AeroShards canvas hard-cutting color at the midline makes "one person, two identities" a felt property of the background, not just a label.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Work hero is effectively invisible on mobile load** -- On a 375x812 viewport, loading `/#work` directly renders almost none of the headline "以系统化思维，做跨境中台与业务架构" or its two CTAs; `WorkImpact` overlays on top of `WorkHero` (independently corroborated by both assessments -- one via code/pin reasoning, one via a live screenshot showing the heading visually overlapping). Why it matters: PRODUCT.md names exactly this scenario -- a recruiter opening the link from a resume/email on a phone. Fix: give WorkHero's ScrollTrigger pin a mobile-safe variant, or disable the layered pin overlap below `md`. Suggested command: /impeccable adapt.

**[P1] Pinned scrollytelling reveals text in unreadable fragments and eats the "minutes" budget** -- WorkImpact and WorkMethodTrace pin the viewport through ~6-8 screen-heights combined, revealing text tied to precise scroll position (confirmed mid-scrub: only disconnected fragments visible). Why it matters: fails "one thing at a time" and works against the stated success behavior. Fix: shorten pin distances further and/or add a visible progress indicator inside the pinned frame. Suggested command: /impeccable distill.

**[P1] Real legibility/contrast defects, concentrated but site-wide** -- Work: body meta text at 3.0:1 contrast (need 4.5:1). Life: seven instances of 11px text and four of 10px functional labels, plus cramped padding on a meta panel. Why it matters: WCAG AA failures and readability floors, hitting the accessibility-dependent persona and anyone skimming on a dim phone screen. Fix: raise meta-text color to >=4.5:1, lift functional labels to an 11px+ floor. Suggested command: /impeccable audit.

**[P2] Unverified placeholder metrics carry the same visual confidence as verified ones** -- "4.8/5" and "42%"/"3.6" render identically to the verified "96%" metric, no inline marker, despite portfolioData.ts TODO-flagging them internally. Why it matters: PRODUCT.md explicitly forbids these reading as fact. Fix: add a subtle "预估" tag or footnote marker on exactly these three numbers. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify.

**[P2] Sole reliance on Geist as the typeface** -- flagged by the CLI scan (4 declarations) and confirmed by both browser scans (95% of Work text, 62% of Life text). Why it matters: the one place the "两个世界" duality currently doesn't have its own voice. Fix: give at least one side (or headings site-wide) a more distinctive face. Suggested command: /impeccable typeset.

**[P3] Horizontal project rail has no directional/count affordance** -- WorkSelectedProjects' scroll-jacked rail converts vertical scroll to horizontal card movement silently. Why it matters: quick-skimming recruiters may not realize more project cards exist. Fix: add a lightweight "1/4" indicator inside the pinned rail. Suggested command: /impeccable layout.

**[P3] Small cosmetic nits** -- one project CTA sits flush against the viewport edge at rest (-15px gap); travel-ticket cards show a card-in-card nesting pattern; the green "glow" shadow on method-trace labels reads as generic "slop" to the detector but is likely an intentional brand accent. Suggested command: /impeccable polish.
